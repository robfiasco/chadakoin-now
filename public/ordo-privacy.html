Ordo Daily — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Ordo Daily is a daily task list for iPhone, built and operated by Chadakoin Digital in Jamestown, NY. It is designed to keep your information on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

Short version

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers of any kind.
  • We never see your tasks.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any to sell or share.

Where your tasks are stored

  • On your iPhone, in the app's private storage.
  • In your iCloud account, if you are signed in to iCloud. Ordo uses Apple's CloudKit private database to sync tasks between your devices. That data belongs to your Apple ID; Chadakoin Digital cannot access it. It is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting the app removes the copy on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your iPhone.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Ordo only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase exists. We never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. Their content never leaves your device.

Permissions we request

  • Notifications — only if you turn on a daily reminder or a task reminder.
  • iCloud — used automatically when you are signed in, to sync your tasks.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, request, or transmit:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any contact info
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers (advertising ID or otherwise)
  • Your tasks, app usage, or behavioral data
  • Photos, contacts, calendar, or microphone input

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone of any age.

Future changes

If this ever changes, we will update this policy clearly, increment the "Last updated" date above, and disclose every category of data and every third party involved before the change ships.
